    Valid Student ID: Always present validated AdDU Student ID before borrowing tools.

Computer Lab Workstation Rules

D202 PC Usage Standards

  • 01
    No Food or Drinks: Food and open drinks are strictly prohibited near workstation PCs.
  • 02
    Session Logout: Always log out of your workstation session when finished.
  • 03
    PC Monitoring: Unsafe file downloads and unauthorized system changes are strictly monitored.

Hardware & Circuits Safety

D205 Safety Checklist

  • 01
    Circuit Power Off: Power off all bench sources before wiring or modifying circuit breadboards.
  • 02
    Safety Gear: Wear protective equipment (safety glasses) during soldering or high-voltage tests.
  • 03
    Hazard Reporting: Report faulty electrical outlets, exposed conductors, or bench equipment immediately.
